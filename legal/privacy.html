Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sorted mobile application (“Application”) collects, uses, and protects personal information. The Application was developed by Subbulakshmi Arunachalam (“Service Provider”) and offered as a free service for personal use.

Information Collection and Use

The Application stores information you choose to enter so it can provide your account, conversations, journals, and AI help. It does not include a separate analytics tool that records which screens you visit or how long you spend in the Application. No precise location data is collected.

When the Application contacts its service providers over the internet, those providers may receive ordinary connection details, such as an IP address, as part of handling the request. This policy does not claim that the Application saves a history of screens or time spent.

Personal Information

To create an account, you provide an email address and name. You may also choose to save a phone number, date of birth, and pronouns. If you allow notifications, a push token may be saved with your profile.

The following may be stored using Supabase, the service that hosts the Application’s database and file storage:

  • Account information
  • Chats
  • Journals
  • Moods
  • Voice recordings
  • Profile photos

This information is used to operate the Application. The Service Provider does not sell personal information.

AI Features

When you use AI features, the text you submit is sent to Anthropic to generate responses or analysis. This can include AI chat messages, conversation text used to suggest replies, and journal text. The audio of a voice recording is not sent to Anthropic by the current voice handler, as described below.

Voice Recordings and Profile Photos

Voice recordings are stored in Supabase. In the current voice handler, the recording is not transcribed. The handler sends Anthropic a fixed description, “The user recorded a short voice reflection about their emotions today,” and Anthropic uses that description for mood and keyword analysis.

If you add a profile photo, the image file is stored in Supabase.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses Supabase to store account information and files, and Anthropic to generate AI responses from text you submit. These providers operate under their own privacy policies. The Application does not use a separate analytics provider to track screens visited or time spent.

Data Retention

Information is kept so the Application can provide your account and the features you use. This policy does not state a fixed number of days for how long information is kept.

You can start account deletion in the Application under Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. You can also request deletion by emailing sortedchatapp@gmail.com without reinstalling the Application.

The current in-app deletion process is written to delete the login and related database records, including chats and journals. In the current code, that process does not delete voice recordings or profile photos stored as files, and deletion of those files has not been verified. An email request can ask for those files to be removed as well. This policy does not promise how quickly a request will be completed.

Security

Access to the Application requires an account. Conversations are stored so the Application and its AI features can read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted. No online system is completely secure, and this policy does not promise that stored information cannot be accessed through the service.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under 13. If you enter a date of birth, sign-up rejects a date that shows an age under 13. Date of birth is optional, so this check does not run when the date is left blank. Parents or guardians who believe a child has shared data should contact sortedchatapp@gmail.com for removal.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will appear on this page, and continued use of the Application indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact the Service Provider at sortedchatapp@gmail.com.
